    Methodology, BadgerNet Validation Rules & Automated Reconciliation (__NPASS__ / __NCHK__ checks PASS · built __BUILD__)

    BadgerNet Validation & Denominators

    • Headline validation basis: Includes all NICU admissions with complete, cross-checked BadgerNet episode records (valid gestation, birthweight, 1-hour admission temperature and maternal history), excluding short (<4h) transitional care observations.
    • Raw submission rate: Shown on the BadgerNet KPI card (Table 5 basis, including <4h transitional care episodes) to distinguish total data capture from validated audit completeness.
    • Overlapping interventions: Babies achieving both admission normothermia (36.5–37.5°C) and deferred cord clamping (≥1 min) count in both measures.

    Statistical Disclosure Control

    • Rounding rule: In line with NHS England and RCPCH NNAP suppression standards, all episode counts are rounded to the nearest 5.
    • Small cohorts: Unit-cohort cells with fewer than 23 validated episodes are suppressed as too few to rate; cells with 23 to 49 episodes are marked * as indicative.
    • Sum-of-parts denominator: Clinical cohort rates use the sum of recorded pathway cells rather than the independently rounded total, preventing small-group percentages from exceeding 100%.

    Reproducible Pipeline & Reconciliation

    • End-to-end build: Rebuilt from raw zipped CSV extracts via filter_rows.sh → clean.py → dims.py → dropzeros.py → dash.py → ext.py → extend.py → validate.py → build_html.py.
    • Independent verification: validate.py reconciles every JSON figure against the raw extract tables (__NPASS__ of __NCHK__ checks passing within rounding tolerance).
